☕ THE CAFETIÈRE SURVIVAL GUIDE

A mandatory workplace safety document
commissioned entirely because of one person.
📋 INCIDENT REPORT — THE KITCHEN, TUESDAY

Ashleigh approached the cafetière with confidence. She pushed the plunger. The cafetière did not plunge. It squirted — launching boiling coffee sideways onto her left hand. The cafetière feels nothing. This document is why.

1
PRE-HEAT THE GLASS

Swirl hot water in the empty cafetière, then discard. Cold glass + boiling water = shattering glass = new incident report. We're running low on forms.

2
COARSE GROUNDS ONLY

Fine grounds clog the filter, build pressure, and that pressure goes sideways into your colleague. Fine grounds = The Squirt. We are calling it The Squirt now. Officially.

3
WAIT 30 SECS AFTER BOIL

~94°C is ideal. Pour slowly in a circle over the grounds. Don't pour it like you're angry. Use 1 heaped tbsp per 100ml. The cafetière will remember your energy.

4
LID ON. WALK AWAY.

One stir, plunger up, lid on. Set a timer — 4 minutes exactly. Then LEAVE. Do not hover. Do not check at 45 seconds. It's fine. Go away.

⚠ THIS IS THE ONE. READ THIS ONE. ⚠
5
THE PLUNGE — YES, THIS ONE

Slow. Steady. Gentle. Feel resistance? STOP. Do not push harder. Resistance = pressure = THE SQUIRT = Ashleigh's hand = this sign. Stir and retry. Or have tea.

6
POUR INTO THE MUG

Pour immediately — waiting makes it bitter and philosophical. Aim for the mug. The mug has a handle so your hand stays away from the hot part. This was intentional. Use it.

🩹 WHEN YOU ARE ASHLEIGH
DOCool running water — 20 full minutes. Not 5. Set a timer. Like you should have for the brew.
DOCover loosely with cling film once cooled. It's a burn, not a parcel.
DOGo to A&E if it's blistering or larger than a 50p coin.
DON'TApply butter, toothpaste, or anything your nan swears by. Wrong. All of it.
DON'TUse cafetière steam to "sanitise" the wound. We cannot believe this needs saying.
DON'TMake another coffee immediately. Sit down. Reflect. Fill in the accident book.
☕ HOW TO NOT BE ASHLEIGH
🔍Check the rubber seal every time. Cracked or missing = do not use. Today is a tea day.
📏Don't overfill. Leave 2–3cm at the top. Overfill = pressure = sideways = hands.
🏳️Resistance means you've already lost. Stop. Have tea. Herbal tea never hurt anyone here.
🧼Clean it after every use. Old grounds = blocked filter = The Squirt. 30 seconds. Do it.
📣If it seems broken, write a note. Don't leave it for the next person. Her name is Ashleigh.
