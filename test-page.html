☕ THE CAFETIÈRE SURVIVAL GUIDE

a workplace safety comic — dedicated to ashleigh's left hand
📋 OFFICIAL INCIDENT REPORT — THE KITCHEN, TUESDAY

At approximately [unknown time, estimated "before she'd had any coffee, which is deeply ironic"], Ashleigh approached the cafetière with confidence. She gripped the plunger. She pushed. The cafetière, sensing weakness, did not plunge. It squirted — launching a pressurised jet of near-boiling coffee sideways directly onto Ashleigh's left hand. Ashleigh is okay. The cafetière feels nothing. It has been returned to active service. This document is why.

⚠️ THE CAFETIÈRE IS NOT YOUR FRIEND. IT IS A GLASS TUBE OF PRESSURISED BOILING LIQUID AND IT HAS ALREADY PROVEN IT WILL USE THIS AGAINST YOU. ⚠️
📖 HOW TO NOT BE ASHLEIGH — A STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
1
PRE-HEAT THE GLASS

Swirl a splash of hot water in the empty cafetière then dump it. Cold glass + boiling water = thermal shock = shattering glass = a whole new incident report. We only have so many pages in the accident book.

Feels pointless. So did reading this guide. Do it anyway.
2
COARSE GROUNDS ONLY

Like sea salt, rough sand, or your will to live on a Monday. Fine grounds clog the filter, build up pressure, and that pressure needs somewhere to go. That somewhere is you. Fine grounds are the direct cause of The Squirt. We are calling it The Squirt now. Officially.

Ashleigh has approved the name "The Squirt." She's being very good about it.
3
WATER: HOT, NOT NUCLEAR

Wait 30 seconds after the kettle boils. ~94°C is ideal. Pour slowly in a circular motion over the grounds — like you're doing a very calm caffeinated blessing. About 1 heaped tablespoon per 100ml. Don't pour it like you're angry at the cafetière. The cafetière will remember.

4
LID ON. WALK AWAY. 4 MINS.

One gentle stir, plunger pulled fully up, lid on. Set a timer for exactly 4 minutes. Then LEAVE. Do not hover. Do not check it at 45 seconds. The coffee is fine. It doesn't need you. Give it its 4 minutes of peace — more than you gave it last time.

It's brewing. It's fine. Go away.
⚠ THIS IS THE ONE. READ THIS ONE. ⚠
5
THE PLUNGE — THE INCIDENT STEP

One palm flat. Slow. Steady. Gentle. Like pressing a lift button. The moment you feel resistance: STOP. Do not push harder. Do not "go for it." Resistance = pressure = THE SQUIRT = Ashleigh's hand = this entire document. If it resists: stir and retry gently. Or have tea.

Ask yourself: is this coffee worth it? Ashleigh knows the answer.
6
POUR INTO THE MUG. THE MUG.

Pour immediately — don't let it sit or it over-extracts into something that tastes like a grudge. Aim for the inside of the mug. The mug has a handle so your hand stays away from the hot part. This was not an accident of design. Use. The. Mug.

Your hand is not a mug. The counter is not a mug. The mug is the mug.
🩹 WHAT TO DO WHEN YOU ARE ASHLEIGH
DOCool running water. 20 minutes. On a clock. Not 5. Not "until it feels better." Twenty. Minutes. Set a timer, like you should have done for the brew.
DOCover loosely with cling film once cooled. It is a burn, not a parcel. Do not wrap it tightly.
DOGo to A&E if it's blistering, larger than a 50p coin, or your gut says "this is bad." Your gut is right.
DON'TApply butter, toothpaste, ice, yoghurt, or anything your nan swears by. None of it works. Nan is wrong about this one specifically.
DON'TUse cafetière steam to "sanitise" the wound. We cannot believe this needs to be written. And yet.
DON'TMake another coffee immediately. Sit down. Reflect. Fill in the accident book. You've been through something.
☕ HOW TO NEVER BE ASHLEIGH
🔍Check the rubber seal every time. The gasket around the plunger is the only barrier between a pleasant morning and The Squirt. If it's cracked, warped, or gone: do not use the cafetière. Today is a tea day.
📏Don't fill it to the brim. Leave 2–3cm at the top. Overfilling means pressure has nowhere to go except sideways, which is where hands typically live.
🏳️If it resists, you have already lost. The cafetière has decided. Stop pushing. Accept it. Herbal teas have never hospitalised anyone in this office.
🧼Clean it after every use. Old grounds clog the filter. Clogged filter = pressure = The Squirt. Rinse it. 30 seconds. It prevents all of this.
📣If it seems broken, TELL SOMEONE. Stiff plunger? Cracked glass? Wobbly lid? Put a note on it. Don't leave it for the next person to discover experimentally. We have a next person. Her name is Ashleigh.
