Deduction Categories
Customer Service Persistent failure to make D-1 calls, and/or any upheld customer complaint. −5%
Fleet Failure to complete Daily Vehicle Checks before starting work, and/or any unreasonable recorded speeding incident. −5%
Housekeeping Starting work after 8am on a working day, and/or refusing work in day. −5%
Compliance — Cat 1 Any Cat 1 failure in the month. −20%
Compliance — Cat 2 Two or more Cat 2 failures in the month. −10%
Compliance — Cat 3 Five or more Cat 3 failures in the month. −10%
Cat 2 + Cat 3 together still deduct 20% (10%+10%) — Compliance is capped at a maximum of 20% deduction regardless of how many Compliance boxes are ticked.
Ticking Fleet or Housekeeping reveals a box for the incident date(s) — enter day numbers (e.g. 3, 15, 22) or full dates, comma-separated.
Fleet is filled in automatically from the vehicle check data uploaded on Gareth Stats: any day an engineer was marked X (In) or H/H (Half Day) but their check wasn't complete is ticked and added to the dates. Add speeding dates alongside them, or remove any you don't want — a missed check you remove and save won't be added back.
Housekeeping is filled in the same way from the Van Start & End data: any working day an engineer's first journey started at 08:00 or later, or their last journey ended before 16:30. Holiday, half-day (H/H) and absence days are skipped. Add refused-work dates alongside them.
