Method. Each job is classed by days_notice: a value of 1 or more is a correct D-1 call, 0 is a call made on the day of the job, and null means the customer was never called. Percentages are shares of all jobs in the selected date range and status filter, per engineer. Jobs are matched on job_reference, so re-uploading an updated file refreshes existing jobs rather than duplicating them.
