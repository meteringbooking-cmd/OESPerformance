How it's counted. Every row in the upload is one job, matched on Ref. When you upload, any Ref already stored is skipped, so only new jobs are added and existing ones are never changed. A job counts as D Minus Made when its CallOutcome reads "D Minus Made" (case and spacing ignored). Weeks use the sheet's Week Beg column, or the Monday of the job date if that's blank. Months go by job date, so a week that spans two months is split between them.
